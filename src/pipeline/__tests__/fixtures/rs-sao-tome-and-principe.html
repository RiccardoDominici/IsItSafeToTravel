SECURITY SITUATION — Security in São Tomé and Príncipe is relatively good and the risk of incidents is generally low, although robberies may occur. In this regard, it is recommended to take care of valuables and money and, where possible, leave them in the hotel.

TRANSPORT — There are two airports in São Tomé and Príncipe: one on São Tomé Island – São Tomé International Airport (TMS), and the other on Príncipe Island – Príncipe Airport (PCP).
To rent a vehicle (rent-a-car), an international driving permit is required. Most roads are in poor condition, especially outside the capital. Driving at night is not recommended.
Minibus transport is mainly used by the local population, while foreign nationals are advised to use taxis (preferably arranged through hotel accommodation).

OTHER INFORMATION — The country calling code for São Tomé and Príncipe is 00 239.
The national currency is the dobra (Db). International credit and debit cards can be used in better hotels and restaurants, and it is advisable to check in advance whether they are accepted. Cash can be withdrawn from a limited number of ATMs. Money transfers can be made via Western Union and other services.
