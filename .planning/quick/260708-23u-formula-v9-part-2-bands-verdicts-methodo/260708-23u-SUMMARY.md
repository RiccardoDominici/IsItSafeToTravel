## Map-Utils / Colors.ts Domain Chosen

`.domain([3.7, 6.5, 8.9])` — danger floor (3.7, near YEM's actual 3.72), distribution centre
(6.5, close to the empirical mean 6.75), excellent ceiling (8.9, near ISL's actual 8.89), applied
identically to `map-utils.ts` (world map) and `colors.ts` (ScoreHero/GlobalScoreBanner/
TrendChart/compare pages) — the latter was an undocumented duplicate discovered via its own
"MUST match map-utils.ts exactly" comment, not listed in the plan's `files_modified`.

## AuthorityLinks Threshold Decision

`country.score >= 6` → `>= 7`, aligning the "safest-first" ranking-link ordering with the new
positive/"good" band boundary under the compressed mean (6.75) — a country at 6.0-6.99 now sits
in the "moderate" band, so ordering "safest" links first for it would have been inconsistent
with its own verdict.

## Jamaica-7.36 Copy Softening

`country-faq-copy.ts` `a1Verdict.low` (×7 locales) softened from an unqualified affirmative
("Yes — {name} is considered a safe destination...") to a hedged one ("Yes, generally —
{name} is considered a safe destination..."). The `>=7` band boundary itself is unchanged from
v8, but v9's compressed range means the "low" band now spans [7.0, 8.89] instead of [7.0, 9.93]
— a country like Jamaica at 7.36 sits much closer to the global mean (6.75) than to the band's
former ceiling, so an unqualified "Yes" risked reading as unreservedly safe for scores just
above the threshold.
